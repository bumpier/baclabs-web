Although Sterile Water and Sodium Chloride (NaCl) water are sometimes used, Bacteriostatic Water, with its unique properties, is often the preferred choice. This post explores why Bacteriostatic Water is the superior peptide reconstitution option. What is Bacteriostatic Water? Bacteriostatic Water is a sterilised form containing a bacteriostatic agent - benzyl alcohol (0.9%). This agent inhibits bacterial growth, giving it an advantage over Sterile Water, which lacks an antimicrobial compound. The benzyl alcohol content inhibits microbial growth, which is why preserved diluents carry a longer in-use shelf life than unpreserved sterile water. Sterile Water is water that has been sterilised and contains no added compounds. As it lacks antimicrobial agents, it is intended for single use. Once exposed, Sterile Water can be contaminated easily, making it less suitable for long-term peptide research. What is NaCl Water? NaCl Water, or Sodium Chloride Water, is a saline solution often used in medical settings. Although it can be used to mix peptides, it lacks the antimicrobial properties of Bacteriostatic Water, and its salt content may potentially influence peptide stability and effectiveness. Why is Bacteriostatic Water the Recommended Choice for Peptides? Prevents Bacterial Contamination: Bacteriostatic Water, with its bacteriostatic agent benzyl alcohol, provides a hostile environment for bacterial growth, making it a preferred option for long-term peptide research. Multiple Use: Unlike Sterile Water, intended for single use, Bacteriostatic Water can be used multiple times within 28 days. This makes it more cost-effective and convenient for research purposes. Easy Availability: Bacteriostatic Water is more straightforward to manufacture and is typically more readily available than Sterile or NaCl Water. In Summary The importance of selecting the appropriate reconstitution medium for peptide research cannot be overstated. Bacteriostatic Water, with its ability to prevent bacterial contamination and multi-use nature, stands out as the superior choice.
